# Business Questions

## Main Research Question

What skills, locations, industries, and experience levels are most in demand for data-related jobs in Australia?

## Supporting Questions

1. Which data-related job titles appear most frequently?
2. Which technical skills are mentioned most often?
3. Which cities and states have the highest number of vacancies?
4. Which industries hire the most data professionals?
5. What salary ranges are offered for different roles?
6. How do skill requirements differ between junior, mid-level, and senior positions?
7. How often are remote and hybrid roles offered?
8. Which combinations of skills appear together most frequently?
9. Which employers publish the largest number of vacancies?
10. What skills should an entry-level data analyst prioritize to improve employability in Australia?

## Target Roles

- Data Analyst
- Business Analyst
- BI Analyst
- Reporting Analyst
- Insights Analyst
- Junior Data Analyst
- Analytics Consultant

## Expected Outcome

The project will identify the most valuable skills, locations, industries, and career paths for people seeking data-related roles in Australia.
